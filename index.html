How an automation works.

Every system follows the same four steps.

  1. 1

    Trigger

    A schedule, a new email, a form, a change in the data.

  2. 2

    Work

    AI reads, sorts and decides, following your rules.

  3. 3

    Check

    A separate step verifies the result before anything counts as done.

  4. 4

    Deliver

    A report, an update in your tools, or a message to the right person.

Most automations skip step three. It's the one I care about most, because an automation that quietly fails is worse than none at all.
